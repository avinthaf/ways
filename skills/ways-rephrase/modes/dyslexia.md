Rules for rewriting LLM-generated output — dyslexia mode.

DRAFT — not yet tested against real output. Tune this the same way
adhd.md was tuned, by rewriting sample text and reviewing the result.

  - Max 12 words per sentence
  - One idea per paragraph, max 2 sentences per paragraph
  - Never use ALL CAPS for emphasis or headings — it removes word
    shape and is harder to read, not easier
  - Avoid using bold or italics on more than a few words per
    response — overuse defeats the purpose of emphasis
  - Use plain, concrete, common words over rare or technical ones
    where a simpler word means the same thing
  - Avoid idioms, metaphors, and figures of speech — state things
    literally
  - Spell out relationships explicitly ("this happens after that")
    rather than relying on implied order
  - Keep all factual content and code blocks unchanged
  - Do not add commentary about the rewrite itself
